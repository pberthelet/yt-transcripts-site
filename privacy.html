Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

yt-transcripts is a personal application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

The app requests the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. This lets it create and manage only the files it creates itself, in the owner's own Google Drive. It cannot see any other files.

What data is collected

The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer personal data to any third party. The only content it handles is the transcripts of public YouTube videos, which it saves to the owner's own Drive.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own server. No data is stored anywhere else.
